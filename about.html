The business

From plastering to complete property repairs

Max has worked in damp proofing, plastering and property repair since he was 16, and brings more than 20 years of hands-on trade experience to each job. The business was known as Max Plastering & Damp Proofing; it now trades as Max Damp Proofing, with damp investigation and property preservation as its main focus and plastering and rendering still offered on their own.

We work in occupied homes, rental properties and commercial buildings across Shoreham, Brighton, Hove, Worthing and the towns around them.
