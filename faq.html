Is the first visit free?

Yes. The initial visit is free. Detailed written reports may be chargeable, and the scope and fee are agreed before you commit.

Do you only do damp, or can I book plastering on its own?

Plastering and rendering are services in their own right. A room skim, a ceiling repair or external render can be booked directly, with no damp problem needed.

Is it rising damp?

Sometimes, but often it is not. Penetrating damp, condensation, leaks and high ground levels cause similar marks. A moisture reading alone does not prove a failed damp proof course, so we find the source first.
