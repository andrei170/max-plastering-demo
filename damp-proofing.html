Damp Proofing

Treat the cause, not the stain

A high moisture reading on its own does not prove a failed damp proof course. We look at the construction, previous repairs, ground levels, gutters and ventilation, then recommend the work that addresses the source. Where plaster has been damaged, we put it right afterwards.
