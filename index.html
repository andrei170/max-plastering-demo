Damp is a symptom, not a diagnosis

Tide marks, salts, peeling paint and a musty smell tell us where to look. They do not tell us the cause on their own. A Victorian solid-brick terrace in Brighton behaves differently from a cavity-wall home in Lancing, so we look outside as well as in before recommending anything.

  • Rising damp: ground moisture moving up through masonry
  • Penetrating damp: rain getting in through brickwork, render, roofs or windows
  • Condensation: moist air meeting cold surfaces
  • Leaks, high ground levels and blocked gutters, which keep walls wet
